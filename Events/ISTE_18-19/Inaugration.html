A START OF NEW JOURNEY : INAUGURATION AND HANDOVER CEREMONY OF 2018-19 ISTE COUNCIL

  • By :
  • 23rd August, 2018

On 23rd August, 2018, inauguration and handover ceremony was conducted in seminar room of A building. It started with curtain rasing ceremony of new logo with the help of drone.All the previous year member were given a letter of appreciation and new year council were given their post and responsibilities for the current year. The event ended with a group photo with previous year members and new year members.The batons were passed to new council to further carry on legacy of council and to raise standard to new levels.
