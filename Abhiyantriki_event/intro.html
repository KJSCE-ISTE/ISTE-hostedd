You, the Technocrats, are on a mission to collect all the infinity stones for safekeeping. But you must hurry, since your race is not just against time but also against the Titan Thanos! Acquire all the stones before the Mad Titan, and save your universe!

We, the watchers will be your guides through this vast universe. We will intervene no more than thrice to help you on your journey.

* Buckle up and get ready with a pen and a paper *
GODSPEED!

HOW TO PLAY ?

  • Solve all the given questions and note down your answers.
  • Read the instructions for the passcode and find the letters required.
  • Rearrange the letters to form a word which is described in the last instruction.
  • Replace the name before .html in the url with the word you have created in all caps.
  • This will lead you to the next stone.
  • Repeat these instructions until you have acquired all the stones.

Scoring and Hints!

  • Every question is worth 4 marks
  • You will get 3 hints and 2 answer reveals.
  • A hint will cost you 2 marks
  • An answer reveal will cost you 4 marks

After acquiring all stones!

  • After you have acquired all the stones, submit the provided google form.
  • Your run at the competition is finished when you submit the google form.
  • The team with the highest score will win.
