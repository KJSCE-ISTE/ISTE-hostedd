Tech Quiz

A quiz competition comprising of MCQs based on engineering subjects for individual participants, divided in 3 different rounds to test your swiftness in giving correct answers.

Tech Quiz
A quiz competition comprising of MCQs based on engineering subjects for individual participants, divided in 3 different rounds to test your swiftness in giving correct answers. Know more: A quiz competition comprising of MCQs based on engineering subjects for individual participants and divided in 3 different rounds with increasing difficulty level. It will have 3 rounds where each round will be considered an elimination round.

Round 1

  • Round 1 will have 12 questions.
  • Difficulty level: Easy
  • Top teams scoring maximum points will be promoted to round 2.

Round 2

  • The points scored in round 1 will be carried forward to round 2.
  • Round 2 will have 12 questions.
  • Difficulty level: Medium
  • Top teams scoring maximum points will be promoted to round 3.

Round 3

  • For round 3, the points scored in previous rounds will NOT be considered.
  • Round 3 will have 12 questions.
  • Difficulty level: Hard
  • Top 3 teams scoring maximum points will be awarded 1st, 2nd and 3rd prizes respectively.

NOTE:

  • 1000 points will be given to the quickest answer in the 1st and 2nd and 3rd round. Points awarded for correct answers will decrease for subsequent slower answers in all rounds

Requirements:

  • Laptop and/or smartphone.
  • Good and stable internet connection for quiz and online meeting.

Registration Fees: Rs 20/- individual

Where : MS Teams
When : 21 April,2021
Time: 3pm - 5pm
