ABHIYANTRIKI 2018
ESCAPE FROM THE LABYRINTH
PRESENTED BY ISTE

OVERVIEW:
There will be a Labyrinth or call it a Maze with different entry points and one exit point.
A camera will be mounted on a BOT which will be running inside the maze.
The participant has to control the bot by a smartphone or remote through the live stream from inside the maze.
A track of time will be kept for each participant.
The participant who reaches the exit in the shortest amount of time will be the winner.


RULES:
The event will be comprising of some substages.
Time limit shall be there. If the participants fail to reach the EXIT of the maze in the predetermined amount of time they will be eliminated/disqualified.
Total time taken will be the sum of all the time taken to complete every substage.
It will be individual/group task. (yet to be determined)
No participants will be allowed to peep in the real maze, the only way to access the maze will be through camera feed. The maze shall be covered by all the sides for the same.
Decision of the organizers will be final.

SUBSTAGES:

There will be some Riddle/Puzzle/Codes/I.Q. Questions at some intervals in the correct path of the maze. By solving them the participant shall get a code for each level solving. All these codes shall combine to form a Master Key, through which the Exit of the maze shall open.
The exit point shall have a code lock system.
