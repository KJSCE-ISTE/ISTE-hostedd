Ranking based Sentence Retrieval for Text Summarization

Abhishek Mahajani
Isaac Maria
Vinay Pandya

Ranking based Sentence Retrieval for Text Summarization

Text Summarization is the technique of extricating notable data from the first content archive. In this procedure, the separated data is produced as a consolidated report and introduced as a clearly expressed rundown. Extractive Summarization technique includes choosing critical text or themes from the content and compiling it into a shorter frame. The significance of sentences is chosen in light of measurable and semantic highlights of sentences. The proposed paper delineates the procedure for Text Summarization using the Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR) scoring methodology. The system identifies the most relevant words and selects the sentences which are similar to the query, generated by the words. This automated text summarization algorithm is capable of re-ranking the sentences from the archive while taking into consideration the semantics and producing a shorter content capable of representing the original content.

Automated Gait Generation for Simulated Bodies using Deep Reinforcement Learning

Abhishek Ananthakrishnan
Dipen Ved
Vatsal Kanakiya

Automated Gait Generation for Simulated Bodies using Deep Reinforcement Learning

A popular problem to solve these days is to propose an algorithm such that a body autonomously learns locomotion. Some of the most efficient and popular contemporary algorithms are Deep Reinforcement Learning algorithms. In this paper, we study three such algorithms from recent times, namely - Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient, Advantage Actor Critic, and Proximal Policy Optimization. We implement and compare the algorithms on the performance metric of average reward per epoch. Given our implementations, we then draw our conclusions on the efficiency of the algorithms proposed and rank the algorithms based on the same.

A.I and Machine Learning Consolation Prize
Interviews based on candidates’ vocal analysis

Hasti Sawla
Vineet Kothari
Krina Joshi

Interviews based on candidates’ vocal analysis

​Face to face interview is a traditional mode of interviewing where interviewer and the job candidate are supposed to be present in the same physical space. There has been a growing interest in understanding what factors are assessed in the employment interview and the properties of those assessments. It is a key issue in organizational psychology and can be addressed as a social computing problem. We approach the problem from a face-to-face, nonverbal perspective where we propose a system which will assess how well did the candidate speak during the interview based on the candidates’ voice. This paper presents a computational framework for the automatic prediction of the speaking ability of the person through vocal analysis, that we refer as Oration Index. Our framework automatically predicts the ratings for constructs like Confidence, Fluency, Energetic and Clarity with an accuracy of 70% and higher, that constitutes to be the major four attributes of the Oration Index. At the last step, we analyse and find the important vocal attributes that contribute in prediction of Oration Index attributes. Thus, our proposed framework will evaluate the speaking performance of the interviewee based on his/her voice using various multi-classification algorithms.

Optimization of continuously variable transmission for an all-terrain using tunable components

Shithija Shetty
Yash Bhatt
Yash Nadagire

Optimization of continuously variable transmission for an all-terrain using tunable components

In the present automobile industry the main focus is on improving the fuel efficiency by making cars lighter and bring improvements in engine, one more aspect that can contribute to the same objective would be the transmission of the car. Sometimes there is a need to shift from fuel efficiency to a good acceleration especially in SUVs and ATVs, continuously variable transmission is an alternative that will achieve both the objectives. In this paper, CVT one of the automatic transmissions is explained along with its components and tuning parameters. By balancing the forces of CVT the equation for engagement rpm, clutching rpm and shift rpm are derived. This paper proposes two mechanisms that would help the rider for quick tuning to achieve favorable performance characteristics from the car. The mathematical model using these equations is prepared to simulate the performance. These results were validated by testing the car with DAQ to measure high end performance.

Automobile 2nd Prize
Yaw moment Diagram

Umang Shah
Varun Kotian
Yash Chauhan
Vivek Mehta

Yaw moment Diagram

The normalized yawing moment v/s acceleration or Cn-Ay graphs is constant velocity graph. It is an envelope of the manoeuvring capability of the car and gives valuable information regarding the steady state characteristics of the car. Excel based simulations were performed in order to develop normalized yawing moment v/s acceleration graphs. The final Cn–Ay graph incorporated a 4 wheel model, weight transfer, variable camber and parallel Ackermann. The graphs are utilised for selecting vehicle design parameters and making setup changes in FSAE cars. Extrapolation of data from yaw moment diagram is used to find the control and stability of vehicle. The yaw moment diagram are validated during the testing phase by logging the data from various sensors and incorporating them in different equations to find the actual value of yaw moment and lateral acceleration.

Rohan Naik
Nilesh Jha
Harsh Ghelani

A Novel Realization of Reversible Configurable Linear Feedback Shift Register

The presented paper focuses on the implementation of a Reversible Configurable Linear Feedback Shift Register using Verilog and evaluates its performance parameters with conventional implementation in the aspect of power dissipation. The practical implementation is configurable in terms of Number of Bits, Seed Value, Number of Taps and Tap Position that allows an increase of randomness of the output. The reversible logic enhances the power efficiency than the conventional models and thus eases the migration to emerging technologies of Quantum Computing and Low Power VLSI. The chosen target for the hardware realization of the Reversible CLFSR is Altera Cyclone II DE-1 Development Board. Furthermore, simulation and synthesis of the design is done on ModelSim-Altera for Quartus II 12.1 Web Edition.

Others Consolation Prize
Performance analysis of solar multi crop

Mitul Yogesh Doshi
Aman R Jain

Performance analysis of solar multi crop

In India open sun crop drying is very popular technique for drying, however solar air dryers are used for crop drying in many places. The solar air dryers are designed and fabricated for a particular crop and have limited usage. The solar air dryers are available in different sizes and configurations. The solar dryer performance depends on various input parameters such as inlet temperature of the air, type of absorber material, type of configuration of solar air dryer, mass flow rate of air, overall heat transfer coefficient, solar radiation, etc. The present article discusses the effects of various parameters on the useful heat gain, instantaneous thermal efficiency of solar air dryer, temperature rise with change in mass flow rate of air, input air temperature, overall heat transfer coefficient and solar air collector area. It is observed that the optimum mass flow rate of air for maximum useful heat gain is 0.05 kg/s. Further, increase in the area will not yield any advantage for overall heat gain. The instantaneous thermal efficiency for various mass flow rates is around 88%. The overall heat transfer effect increases the instantaneous thermal efficiency up to 0.1 kg/s air mass flow rate and thereafter no significant change in the efficiency. The input air temperatures do not yield much effect on the useful heat gain and rise in temperature.
