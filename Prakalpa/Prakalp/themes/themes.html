• Conference

    Conference on Artificial Intelligence on 31 March 2017

    The theme of this year’s Prakalpa is Artificial Intelligence-Innovation towards Future. Papers are invited from students based on any one of the following categories. Send the paper via e-mail only for scrutiny in standard two column IEEE format. In covering letter of e-mail quote your affiliation and the category. A maximum of 4 students can be present in a group
    1. Machine Learning
    2. Computer Vision
    3. Natural Language processing
    4. Data Mining
    5. Artificial Neural Networks
    6. Evolutionary Algorithm

  • Technical Quiz

    Technical Quiz competition on 31st. March 2017.

    This year a technical quiz is being introduced for students from all branches. The quiz will be conducted in 2 rounds, with both being held on the same day.
    The first round will be the elimination round where students from each branch will be asked questions with respect to their branch itself. The top 2 teams from each branch qualify for the final round.
    The final round will have teams facing questions which will be based on the latest as well as on the technical developments which revolutionized the lives of humans.
    A maximum of 3 students can be present in a team.
    The team with the maximum points in the final round will be declared the winner.
    The decision of the organizer will be final in this regard. All rights are reserved with organizers to disqualify entry at any level

  • Working Model Exhibition

    Working Model/Project Exhibition/Competition on 1st. April 2017

    This competition is open to FE to ME students from engineering colleges.
    Send the synopsis of project in any diverse field via e-mail for scrutiny.
    For the evaluation of this competition there will be separate category for FE/SE/TE and BE/ME, each with software and hardware based projects.
    Please mention year and type of project (software/hardware) in e-mail clearly while sending synopsis.
    Same group with multiple entries on same topic/project/theme in all competitions is not permitted.
    A maximum of 4 students can be present in a group
